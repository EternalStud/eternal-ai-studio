# Strategy & Unit Economics — What Actually Earns vs Hype

## Why 95% of "AI Side Income" Projects Earn ₹0
1. **Low-effort AI sludge gets penalized:** Stock footage + ChatGPT script + default TTS triggers YouTube "Inauthentic/Repetitious Content" demonetization and gets <5% retention.
2. **Shorts AdSense alone is a trap in India:** ₹5–₹30 per 1,000 Shorts views means 1,000,000 views = ₹5,000–₹30,000. You cannot rely on Shorts RPM alone.
3. **SEBI / Data-licensing walls:** Selling public stock tips or scraped financial data is legally dangerous.

### Honest Pass/Fail Criteria for Every Prototype
Before uploading or building a full channel automation around any prototype, it must pass **4 gates**:
1. **Lived-In Hook (First 5 Seconds):** Does the opening drop the viewer straight into a physical story (undersea/underground cable, chai tapri, dark archive) rather than generic graphics?
2. **Audio & Pacing:** Is the Hindi/English voice calm, human, conversational, and paired with restrained cinematic SFX?
3. **Full Understanding (`90–150s` Sweet Spot):** Does the viewer leave thinking *"Now I actually understand how that works"* without rushing past the "why"?
4. **Render Budget on Mac Mini M2:** Always pass a 15-second `--spot-only` visual inspection before committing to full multi-minute renders (<25 min full video locally).
